🔬 Microgravity Combustion Physics Insights

In microgravity (g ≈ 0), buoyant convection disappears. Flames become spherically symmetric and are governed entirely by molecular diffusion and radiative heat loss.

🛡️ Solid Flammability (BASS-II Data)

  • Nomex & SIBAL Fabrics: Showed >96% self-extinction in microgravity due to low surface heat retention.
  • PMMA (Acrylic): Only 26% self-extinction; thick slabs sustained 100% combustion.
  • Critical Airspeed: Ventilation speeds between 25-50 display units supply oxygen without cooling the flame, representing the peak hazard zone.

💧 Droplet Combustion (FLEX Data)

  • Helium (He): Highly conductive (6x N₂), boosting flame burning rate K (+0.40 correlation).
  • Carbon Dioxide (CO₂): Heavy radiative emission drops flame temperature and triggers premature extinction (-0.37 correlation).
  • Cool Flame Transition: In heptane droplets, visible hot flame extinguishes while an invisible low-temperature cool flame continues to consume fuel!
