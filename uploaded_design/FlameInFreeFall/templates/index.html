THE MISSION

Predict the fire before it ignites.

01

No up, no down

Buoyancy vanishes. Hot gas stops rising, so flames wrap into spheres fed only by slow molecular diffusion — behaving nothing like fire on Earth.

02

No way out

A spacecraft is a sealed lifeboat. The crew can't evacuate or wait for help, and cabin airflow can either feed a fire or blow it out. Prevention is the only defense.

03

No room for guesswork

Every ISS experiment is rare and costly. Our models learn from 1,205 real runs to estimate outcomes for untested settings — and flags every estimate that leaves the data. A research aid for future Moon and Mars missions.
